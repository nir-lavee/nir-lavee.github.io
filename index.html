About

I am a PhD student at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, under the supervision of Nati Linial. I have broad research interests in combinatorics and discrete algorithms. I also work as a TA for the Computability & Complexity course.

Previously: my MSc advisor was Orna Kupferman, I completed it in 2022 (summa cum laude). Before doing research, I worked at the Israeli olympiad in informatics, where I helped coach students for the IOI.
